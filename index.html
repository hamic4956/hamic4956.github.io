🎓 Canvasist - Never Miss Another Assignment Deadline

Canvasist is a tiny, private, open-source Windows app that pulls every assignment from Canvas and Gradescope into one clean list, then reminds you before it's due. No more tab-hopping. No more "I thought that was due Friday."

Download Canvasist for Windows

Free • Open source • Runs on your own computer

🧭 What Canvasist Actually Does

Most students juggle two or three websites just to find out what is due. Canvas says one thing, Gradescope says another, and somewhere in between an announcement quietly changes a date. Canvasist fixes that by collecting all of it in one place.

When you open Canvasist, you get a single, simple list. Every assignment, quiz, discussion post, and Gradescope submission appears together, sorted by the moment it is due. The next deadline is always at the top, so you never have to go hunting for it.

The app sits quietly in your system tray while you study, and pops up a friendly reminder before something is about to close. That's the whole idea: less checking, fewer surprises.

✨ Why Students Keep It Installed

📚 One list for Canvas and Gradescope

Two platforms, one feed. Canvasist merges your deadlines from both sources and shows them in a single chronological view, so a Gradescope lab and a Canvas discussion never compete for your attention in separate browser tabs.

⏰ Reminders that arrive before it's too late

You choose how much warning you want — a day ahead, a few hours, or both. Canvasist sends a normal Windows notification with the assignment name and the exact due time. You can adjust the timing for each reminder type, or snooze one when you're in the middle of something.

🔍 Everything important at a glance

Each item shows its class, its platform, and its due date. Upcoming work is grouped clearly, and anything overdue is highlighted so it can't hide at the bottom of a long list. A quick scan in the morning tells you exactly where you stand.

🪶 Tiny and light on your laptop

Canvasist is built with Rust and Tauri, which means a small download, a fast launch, and very little memory in use while it runs in the background. It won't slow down your machine during a lecture or while you're writing a paper at 2 a.m.

🔒 Private by design

Your assignment data and your login details stay on your own computer. There is no Canvasist account to create and no server collecting your grades. Because the project is open source, anyone can inspect exactly what the app does and how it handles your information.

🆓 Free and open source

No subscription, no premium tier, no ads. Canvasist is a student-friendly project maintained in the open, and the full source code is available for anyone who wants to learn from it or help improve it.

🚀 Setting It Up for the First Time

The first launch is a short, guided setup. Nothing technical is required, and you can always come back to these settings later from the app menu.

Step 1 — Connect your Canvas account

Canvasist asks for your school's Canvas web address, the same one you type into your browser to reach your courses. It then opens your school's normal sign-in page, where you log in exactly as you always do. Canvasist never asks for your password directly and never stores it.

Step 2 — Connect your Gradescope account

The same idea applies here. You sign in through the regular Gradescope page in your own browser, and Canvasist is given permission to read your course list and assignment dates. That permission is limited to reading deadlines — it is not there to change anything, submit anything, or post on your behalf.

Step 3 — Pick your reminder timing

Choose how far ahead you want to be warned. Many students use two reminders: one the evening before, and one a couple of hours before the deadline. You can change this at any time, and you can pause reminders during quiet hours if you study late.

Step 4 — Let it run in the background

Once your accounts are connected, Canvasist keeps your list fresh automatically and stays out of your way in the system tray. Double-click the tray icon whenever you want to see your deadlines.

🔔 How the Reminders Behave

Canvasist uses standard Windows notifications, which means reminders appear the same way your email or calendar alerts do. Each notification names the assignment, the course, and the exact time it closes. You can click a reminder to jump straight to that assignment in your browser, or dismiss it and get back to work.

If a deadline is changed by your instructor, Canvasist picks up the new date on its next refresh and adjusts your reminders to match. You do not need to delete or re-create anything by hand.

Reminders only fire while the app is running. If you close it completely, notifications stop until you open it again — so for most people, leaving it in the tray is the right choice.

🔐 Privacy and Your Data

Everything Canvasist knows about your courses lives in a local file on your own PC. There is no cloud sync, no telemetry dashboard, and no third-party analytics service bundled in. The app talks to Canvas and Gradescope only to read your assignments, and it talks to nothing else.

If you want to disconnect an account, you can log out from inside Canvasist, which removes the stored access from your computer. You can also revoke access from your school's Canvas account settings at any time, just like any other connected app.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Canvasist really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no trial period, and no advertising. It is an open-source project built for students.

Do I have to give Canvasist my password?

No. You sign in on your school's official Canvas and Gradescope pages, in your own browser. Canvasist receives a limited permission token, which it stores on your computer so it can read your deadline list.

Will it work with my school?

Canvasist works with any institution that uses Canvas or Gradescope, including schools that sign you in through a single university login page. If you can reach your courses in a browser, you can connect them.

Does it submit my assignments for me?

No. Canvasist is read-only. It collects deadlines and reminds you about them. All submitting still happens on Canvas or Gradescope, where it belongs.

What happens when a semester ends?

Finished courses simply stop producing new deadlines and drop out of your active view. Your history stays on your computer until you clear it. If you stop using Canvasist entirely, uninstalling it removes the local data.

🛠️ If Something Isn't Working

My assignments aren't showing up. Open the app and press refresh. If the list is still empty, check that you're signed in to both platforms under Settings — an expired session is the most common cause.

Canvasist is built for students, by people who got tired of missing things. It is written with Rust and Tauri on the desktop side, with a React interface, and it is published as open source so it can be trusted, audited, and improved by the people who use it.

If you find a bug or have an idea for a feature, the repository is the right place to share it. If you simply find the app useful, telling a classmate is the best way to help it grow.
